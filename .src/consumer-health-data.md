**Version 1.1.** Effective November 16, 2026. This policy is written for the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and Connecticut's consumer health data rules, and we apply it to everyone who uses Strata. Version 1.1 adds the body weight you enter, the profile details you can choose to add, and the strength comparison.

## 1. Consumer health data the app processes

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health data you allow from the Apple Health app | Sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, workouts, weight | Your daily readiness estimate and Coach's call |
| Daily check-ins | How you feel (Great, Good, Sore, Stressed, Drained) and any sleep hours you type. Sleep from the Apple Health app is read each time and not stored | Setting your day: full plan, lighter day or a walk |
| Body measurements | Body weight you enter (in your profile or body information). When scan features are available: scan numbers from InBody, DEXA or a smart scale; a photo of the report you attach | Showing how your body changes, and comparing your squat, bench press and deadlift with published powerlifting results |
| Profile details you choose to add | Sex and birth year, each optional | Choosing the comparison group for your squat, bench press and deadlift |
| Training | Workouts, sets, effort, activities | Coaching, progress and recovery estimates |
| Inferences | Readiness estimate, fatigued muscle groups, lighter-day suggestions, strength comparison band | Adjusting your training |

## 2. Sources

You, and the Apple Health app when you allow each data type.

## 3. Where it is processed

Only on your device. Strata Studios LLC does not operate servers for app data and does not receive this information. If you email us, please leave out health details; if you include them, we use them only to reply, and delete them as our [Privacy Policy](/strata/privacy) describes.

## 4. Your consent

The app asks for your consent separately from the Terms of Use, with one switch for reading the Apple Health app and one for daily check-ins and readiness. Both start off. You can withdraw either at any time in More › Privacy & security; the app then stops that use. We don't ask for consent to share, because we don't share. Workouts, body measurements and the training estimates in section 1 don't have a separate switch because they're needed to provide the training features you ask for. You can delete them at any time.

## 5. Sharing and sale

None. We don't share consumer health data with any third party or affiliate, and we don't sell it. There are no trackers, advertising or analytics tools in the app, and we don't use geofencing.

## 6. Your rights

You can see, export and delete this data in the app at any time (More › Privacy & security). You can also email privacy@stratastudios.app to ask about your rights, to confirm deletion of any email you sent, or to appeal a decision. You can correct your information at any time by editing it in the app. We respond within 45 days and never require you to create an account. If we deny your appeal, we'll tell you how to contact the Washington Attorney General (atg.wa.gov) or the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov).

## 7. Changes and tracking

If we change this policy in a material way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect and ask for your consent before any new collection or use. No third party collects consumer health data through the app or our website over time or across other sites or services.
