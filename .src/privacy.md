**Version 1.1.** Effective November 16, 2026.

**The short version: your workout, body and health information stays on your phone. We don't collect it, sell it, share it or use it for ads. We can't see it.**

This policy explains how Strata Studios LLC ("Strata Studios," "we") handles information in the Strata app and on stratastudios.app.

## 1. What the app keeps on your device

The app stores and processes the following only on your phone, to give you the features you use:

| Information | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workouts you log | Exercises, sets, reps, weights, effort, notes, plans, goals | Logging, history, records, plans and Coach suggestions |
| Body information you enter | Body weight and focus areas. When scan features are available: scan results (InBody, DEXA, scale) and scan photos you attach | Body map and Coach suggestions (scan comparisons, when available) |
| Profile details you choose to add | Sex, birth year and body weight, in More › Profile & units. Each is optional, and you can pick "Rather not say" | Showing how your squat, bench press and deadlift compare with other lifters of your sex, age and body weight. The comparison is calculated on your phone |
| Health data you allow | From the Apple Health app: sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, workouts, weight. Read and shown each time they're needed; the app doesn't store these readings | Readiness score and the "what changed" summary |
| Daily check-ins (if you turn them on) | How you feel (Great, Good, Sore, Stressed, Drained) and any sleep hours you type. Sleep from the Apple Health app is read each time and not stored | Coach's call for the day and lighter-day adjustments |
| Activities you log | Runs, rides, classes, sports, walks: type, minutes, how hard | Calendar, weekly goal and leg recovery |
| Your choices and agreements | When you accepted the Terms, your health-data choices, your automatic-renewal confirmation, reminder settings | Proof of what you agreed to, and honoring your choices |
| Settings | Color, units, experience level, goal, days per week | Personalizing the app |

The app reads only the Apple Health app data types you allow, and you can turn each off at any time in your phone's settings or in the app (More › Privacy & security). If you allow it, the app also saves the workouts you finish to the Apple Health app (the type of workout, when it started and how long it lasted). It never writes health measurements.

**We do not receive any of this information.** It is not sent to our servers (we don't have any for app data) or to iCloud, and it is not combined with information from other sources about you.

## 2. What we may receive

- **Purchases:** Apple processes payments. We receive sales reports from Apple that don't include your health data. We do not receive your payment card details.
- **Feedback, votes and support emails:** If you send feedback, vote on features or email support, your email app sends us your email address, what you write, any screenshot you attach, the app version and build and the topic you picked (in the subject line), and your iPhone model. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, keep it in an inbox protected with two-step sign-in, and delete it within 24 months or sooner if you ask. Please don't include health details you'd rather keep private.
- **Crash reports:** If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't include your workout or health data.
- **Website:** Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
- **Website waitlist:** If you join the waitlist on stratastudios.app, we collect your email address and first name, to send you early access, launch and update emails. We don't ask about health, injuries or medical conditions. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we remove you when you unsubscribe or ask. Our email service provider sends these emails for us under a contract that limits its use of your address to sending them. We keep your waitlist information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We don't sell or share the list.

## 3. What we never do

We never sell your information, share it with advertisers or data brokers, use it to target ads, or use health data for marketing. The app contains no advertising or tracking software.

## 4. Exporting and deleting your data

You control your data because it lives on your device. You can export it from More › Privacy & security as a JSON file you save; that file contains your health information, so store it carefully. To delete everything, use More › Privacy & security › Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes its data from this phone, but not the workouts it saved to the Apple Health app or copies in your phone's backups until those backups are replaced. Your phone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) include app data under Apple's protection; the app itself does not store your data in iCloud. Because we don't hold your app data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side; we'll confirm that in writing if you ask, and delete any emails you sent us.

**Deleted workouts and edit history.** When you edit a workout, the app keeps the earlier values on your phone as its edit history. When you delete a workout, it moves to Recently deleted on your phone, where you can restore it. Edit history and deleted workouts stay only on your phone and are included in any export you make. Delete all my data erases everything, including edit history and Recently deleted, and can also remove the workouts Strata saved to the Apple Health app (a checkbox on the confirm screen, on by default).

## 5. Security

The app stores its data using your phone's built-in iOS encryption. The app's own backup copies and the export files it creates are protected whenever the phone is locked; the live workout data is protected until you first unlock the phone after a restart. Once you save an export somewhere else, that place's protection applies. An optional Face ID or passcode lock hides Strata from anyone else using your unlocked iPhone. Keep your phone's passcode on and your software updated.

If information we are responsible for is ever accessed without authorization, we will notify affected people without unreasonable delay and within 60 days, and notify the Federal Trade Commission and others as the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state laws require.

## 6. Age

The app is for adults 18 and older. Where the app store gives us an age signal (such as Apple's Declared Age Range), the app checks it during setup and again from time to time when it opens, uses it only to apply this rule, and keeps only the date of the last check and whether it passed, never your age or age range; if it says the account belongs to someone under 18, the app doesn't open and stores nothing else, and if under 13 it erases what it stored. Setup never asks for your birth date. If you choose to add your birth year in More › Profile & units, the app uses it only for the lift comparisons in Section 1. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

## 7. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (Washington, Nevada and similar laws)

Our [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/strata/consumer-health-data) (stratastudios.app/strata/consumer-health-data) is a separate notice, linked on our website's home page, in the App Store listing and in the app (More › Help & feedback). In short:

- **Categories:** workouts, body measurements (including scans, when available), and the Apple Health app data you allow (Section 1).
- **Purpose:** to provide the features you request, on your device.
- **Sources:** you, and the Apple Health app with your permission.
- **Where it's processed:** only on your device. It is not sent to us and we can't see it. We still treat this as covered by these laws and apply our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy to it.
- **Sharing:** none. We do not share or sell consumer health data with any third party or affiliate.
- **Your rights:** you can access, export and delete your data in the app at any time (Section 4). You may also contact us at privacy@stratastudios.app to ask about your rights or to appeal any decision.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or port your personal information and to appeal our response. Because app data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app. For anything else, email privacy@stratastudios.app. We won't discriminate against you for using your rights.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, especially to collect anything new, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect and ask your permission where required.
