Help with the app

Email support@stratastudios.app. In Strata, More › Help & feedback opens an email with your app version filled in.

Membership and billing

Apple bills every Strata membership. To cancel, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or More › Membership in Strata, which opens that screen. Cancel at least 24 hours before a renewal or the end of a free trial to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a membership.

Apple handles refunds. Start one from More › Membership in Strata, or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your workouts, body and health information stay on your iPhone. Export or delete everything in More › Privacy & security. Privacy questions and requests: privacy@stratastudios.app. Read the Privacy Policy and the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Legal notices

Notices of disputes and arbitration opt-outs go to legal@stratastudios.app. See the Terms of Use.
